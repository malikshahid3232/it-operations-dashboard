# KPI Definitions

## SLA Compliance

**Formula:** Resolved incidents within SLA / Total resolved incidents.

Used to assess whether service commitments are being met.

## MTTR

**Mean Time to Resolve** measures the average elapsed time between incident creation and resolution.

Lower is generally better, but interpretation should consider incident priority and complexity.

## First Contact Resolution

Percentage of incidents resolved during the initial support interaction without requiring escalation or additional support activity.

## Reopen Rate

Percentage of resolved incidents that are subsequently reopened.

A high reopen rate can indicate incomplete resolution, weak knowledge articles, recurring technical causes, or inadequate validation.

## High-Priority Incidents

For this demonstration:

- P1 = Critical
- P2 = High
- P3 = Medium
- P4 = Low

## Management Interpretation

KPIs should not be evaluated in isolation. For example, a lower MTTR can be achieved by closing simple tickets quickly while leaving complex incidents unresolved. A mature service-management dashboard therefore combines volume, SLA, resolution time, priority, recurrence, and business impact.
